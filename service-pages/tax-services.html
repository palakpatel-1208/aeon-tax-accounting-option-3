Individual & Business Tax Services

Individual tax filings

Individual filings cover the federal return and the state returns that apply to your situation, including multi-state filings when your income or residence reaches more than one state. The goal is an accurate return that stays compliant while optimizing your tax position.

  • Federal individual tax preparation and filing
  • Pennsylvania and other state filings
  • Multi-state filings when more than one state is involved

Business tax filings

Business tax filings follow the same standard: accurate preparation, compliance with federal, state, and local regulations, and a filing approach shaped to the business rather than a generic checklist.

  • Federal business tax preparation and filing
  • State and local business filings
  • Multi-state filings for businesses that operate beyond one state

What working together looks like

Every client has unique goals and circumstances. Aeon Tax takes the time to understand your situation, then delivers a filing plan designed for those needs. You will know exactly what to expect before any work begins, with no unexpected charges.

Support is available virtually or in person, whether you are in Pennsylvania or elsewhere in the country.
